<p>A puzzle game about splitting a stream of marbles into exact fractions.</p>

<p>Lay walnut and maple blocks on an open table. Belts carry marbles; <b>splitters</b> deal them out to up to three exits in strict turn; <b>convergers</b> join up to three lines into one; <b>bridges</b> let lines cross. Marbles come in and go out on the sides of your machine, one per side. Each level asks for exact rates at its outputs, as shares of a full belt: a quarter, three sixteenths, one sixtieth. Loops are allowed, and they are how you reach fractions that halves and thirds alone can't.</p>

<p>Press <b>Check</b> and the exact flow through every block is worked out as fractions, then the build runs for 3000 ticks and the shares are measured. Solved? Chase fewer pieces, a smaller footprint and a smoother flow: improving one can cost another.</p>

<ul>
<li>17 levels, from a quarter to one thirtieth, some with two inputs to split apart and recombine</li>
<li>See where marbles back up: waiting blocks glow red; pause and step one tick at a time</li>
<li>A level builder: set the targets, prove your level solvable, and add it to your levels</li>
<li>Tap any block for its exact share and measured load</li>
<li>Share builds as short text codes</li>
<li>Touch controls: drag from a piece to draw a belt, tap it to rotate or delete it, drag it once selected to move it, Select for groups</li>
<li>Offline, no permissions, no ads, no tracking</li>
</ul>

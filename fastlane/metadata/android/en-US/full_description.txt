<p>A puzzle game about splitting a stream of marbles into exact fractions.</p>

<p>Lay walnut and maple blocks on an open table. Belts carry marbles; <b>splitters</b> deal them out to up to three exits in strict turn; <b>convergers</b> join up to three lines into one. Each level asks for target shares at its outputs: a half, three eighths, a seventh, one seventeenth. Loops are allowed, and they are how you reach fractions that halves and thirds alone can't.</p>

<p>Press <b>Check</b> and the exact flow through every block is worked out as fractions, then the build runs for 3000 ticks and the shares are measured. Solved? Chase fewer parts and a smaller footprint.</p>

<ul>
<li>19 levels, from Halves to Thirtieth, plus a sandbox</li>
<li>A random level with a new target whenever you like</li>
<li>Tap any block for its exact share and measured load</li>
<li>Share builds as short text codes</li>
<li>Touch controls: drag from a piece to draw a belt, tap it to rotate or delete it, drag it once selected to move it, Select for groups</li>
<li>Offline, no permissions, no ads, no tracking</li>
</ul>
